Time domain

TDI 2.0 $X,Y,Z$ in fractional frequency, $d_c=c_2/\nu_0$. Min/max envelope; zoom to load samples from the brick.

Frequency domain

Signal $h_c(f)=2\sqrt{f}\,|\tilde d_c(f)|$ against the day-averaged one-sided noise $n_c(f)=\sqrt{f\,\bar S_{cc}(f)}$.

With $\tilde d_c=\Delta t\,\mathrm{rfft}(w\,d_c)$ (Tukey, $\alpha=0.01$) the per-channel SNR is $\rho_c^2=4\Delta f\sum_k|\tilde d_c(f_k)|^2/\bar S_{cc}(f_k)=\int (h_c/n_c)^2\,\mathrm{d}\ln f$; the network SNR uses the full covariance, $\rho^2=4\Delta f\,\mathrm{Re}\sum_k\tilde{\mathbf d}^\dagger\mathbf C^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf d}$.
